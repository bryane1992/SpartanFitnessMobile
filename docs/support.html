Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate a training plan?

Complete the onboarding flow when you first open the app. GritOS will ask about your goals, experience, equipment, and schedule, then generate a personalized plan automatically.

Can I regenerate my plan?

Yes. Go to Settings and tap "Regenerate Plan." This will create a new plan based on your current profile. Your previous plan data will be replaced.

How do I access the AI Coach?

Tap the red AI button in the bottom right corner of any screen. The AI Coach requires a Pro or Elite subscription.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings and tap the subscription banner at the top. This opens the subscription management screen where you can view, change, or cancel your plan. You can also manage subscriptions directly through iPhone Settings → App Store → Subscriptions.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Go to Settings, tap the subscription banner, and select "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Why isn't my GPS run tracking working?

Make sure you've granted GritOS location permissions. Go to iPhone Settings → GritOS → Location → select "Always" or "While Using the App." GPS tracking requires an active location permission.

How do I update my equipment or goals?

Go to Settings and tap "Edit Equipment" to update your available equipment. After making changes, regenerate your plan to get updated exercise selections.
