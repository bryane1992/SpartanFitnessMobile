Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

GritOS ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — used to create and manage your account

Health & Fitness Data

  • Body weight, height, and fitness level provided during onboarding
  • Workout logs, exercise performance, and personal records
  • WOD scores and activity history
  • Run distance, pace, duration, and GPS route data

Location Data

  • Precise GPS location during GPS run tracking sessions only. Location is not collected in the background except during active run tracking, which requires your explicit permission.

User Content

  • Messages sent to the AI Coach, including workout context and questions
  • Notes and customizations made to your training plan

Purchase Information

  • Subscription status and purchase history, managed through Apple's App Store and RevenueCat. We do not store payment card details.

Usage Data

  • App interactions such as plan generation, workout completions, and coach message usage — used for rate limiting and app improvement

How We Use Your Data

  • To generate and personalize your training plan
  • To power the AI Coach with relevant workout context
  • To track your performance and show progress over time
  • To manage your account and subscription
  • To enforce usage limits based on your subscription tier
  • To improve the app and fix bugs

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — stores your account, workout history, and coach messages securely in a hosted database. Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic (Claude) — powers the AI Coach. Coach messages and workout context are sent to Anthropic to generate responses. Anthropic does not receive your name or email. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription purchases and entitlements. Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store — handles all payment processing. We never see your payment card details. Privacy Policy

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon deletion, your data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

GritOS is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data in a portable format

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the date at the top of this page when we do. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
